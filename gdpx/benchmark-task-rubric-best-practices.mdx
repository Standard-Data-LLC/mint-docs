A good GDPx task asks an agent to finish meaningful work with realistic context and tools. Its verifier rewards the intended outcome, accepts valid alternative methods, and explains failures.

Keep difficulty honest: a broken environment is not a hard task, and one model failure does not establish a useful benchmark.

- [Task format](/gdpx/quickstart): the domain/task tree, provenance, model runs, and baseline runs.
- [Design and grading](/gdpx/benchmark-task-design): the instruction, environment, rubric, and calibration.
- [Task packaging](/gdpx/benchmark-task-package): runtime files, multi-step tasks, identity, and visibility.
- [Evaluation and failure analysis](/gdpx/benchmark-task-evaluation): scored attempts, failure origins, and distributions.
- [Partner delivery](/gdpx/benchmark-task-delivery): the final handoff.
- [QC stages](/qc-stages-for-benchmark): construction, difficulty, evaluation, and release review.
